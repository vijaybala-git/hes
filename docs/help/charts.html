WhyWatt's charts are organized into three groups. Select any chart from the dropdown in each chart panel. Use the [?] button next to the dropdown for a quick summary.

Journey Costs (JC)

Answers: "What will this cost, and is the journey worth it?"

JC-1 — Annual Cost: Your Journey vs. Do Nothing

Journey Costs

Shows the total energy bill (electricity + gas) for each year of the simulation, for both scenarios side by side. The gap between bars in a given year is your annual saving (if your journey bar is shorter) or annual premium (if it's taller).

In early years, your journey may cost more because of install costs. In later years, lower operating costs take over — this is the payback dynamic.

Full chart documentation coming in a future release.

JC-2 — Cumulative Cost & Payback Crossover

Journey Costs

Adds up every year's total bill (operating + CapEx) from year 1 onward. The point where the Journey line dips below the Do Nothing line is the payback year — from that year forward, your journey has spent less in total than doing nothing.

This is often the most compelling chart to show homeowners: "By year 8, you've broken even, and everything after that is savings."

JC-3 — 20-Year Summary Bar

Journey Costs

A single side-by-side bar showing the total 20-year spend for each scenario. The difference is your estimated lifetime savings from electrification.

JC-4 — Annual Cost Breakdown by Appliance

Journey Costs

A stacked bar chart where each segment represents one appliance's share of the annual energy bill. Watch this chart across years to see which swaps have the biggest cost impact — the segment for that appliance shrinks in its swap year.

Rates (R)

Answers: "Where do energy prices come from, and how do they change?"

R-1 — Rate Projection

Rates

Shows electricity and gas rates projected forward from today's PG&E tariff using a compound annual growth rate (CAGR). Three scenarios are available: conservative, moderate (default), and stress. See Rates for the CAGR values and their sources.

Full chart documentation coming in a future release.

R-2 — ACC Seasonal Rate Shape

Rates

Shows the monthly variation in the effective electricity rate under the CPUC's Avoided Cost Calculator (ACC) framework. Summer afternoon peak hours carry the highest effective rate; winter nights carry the lowest. See ACC for methodology details.

Energy Use (EU)

Answers: "How much energy does each appliance actually use?"

EU-1 — Annual Energy Consumption by Fuel

Energy Use

Shows total annual energy consumption in physical units — kilowatt-hours (kWh) for electricity and therms for gas. This shows energy quantity before pricing; changes here directly drive the cost charts above.

Full chart documentation coming in a future release.

EU-2 — Per-Appliance Energy Breakdown

Energy Use

A stacked breakdown of energy consumption by appliance. Compare your journey vs. do-nothing to see which appliance swaps reduce energy use most, and by how much.
