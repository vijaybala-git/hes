What WhyWatt is

WhyWatt? is a home electrification cost simulator for California community advocates. It shows the long-term cost of an electrification journey — replacing gas appliances with electric alternatives over time — compared to doing nothing for 20 years.

The primary audience is electrification advocates running sessions with homeowners: people who want to show, with real numbers, what switching to heat pumps, induction, and electric vehicles actually costs and saves over time.

What it models

WhyWatt simulates a single home running two scenarios in parallel:

  • Your Journey — you choose which appliances to swap, and in which year.
  • Do Nothing — all current appliances stay in place for 20 years.

For each year of the simulation, WhyWatt calculates:

  • Energy consumption for each appliance (kWh for electricity, therms for gas)
  • Energy cost using PG&E tariff rates projected forward with escalation scenarios
  • Capital expenditure when appliances are swapped (install cost minus rebates)
  • Solar and battery savings (net of electricity purchased from the grid)

Methodology

WhyWatt uses a Mesa agent-based simulation framework stepping annually for 20 years. All energy quantities are in native physical units: kilowatt-hours (kWh) for electricity and therms for gas. Monthly sub-calculations are used for HVAC and water heating to preserve seasonal accuracy.

Rate escalation uses compound annual growth rates (CAGR) applied to published base rates. Three scenarios are offered: conservative (4%/yr elec, 4%/yr gas), moderate (7%/yr elec, 8%/yr gas — the default, matching 10-year historical averages), and stress (10%/yr elec, 12%/yr gas, per CEC projections).

Limitations and what WhyWatt does not model

  • Appliance lifespan and end-of-life replacement (only scheduled swaps are modeled)
  • Time-of-use rate optimization (flat rates are used; ACC shape is an option)
  • Income-qualified rebate programs (coming in a future release)
  • Monte Carlo uncertainty bands (deterministic model; future release)
  • Multi-utility support beyond PG&E (future release)

Disclaimer

WhyWatt is a planning tool, not a financial guarantee. Actual costs depend on your specific appliances, usage patterns, utility rate changes, local rebate availability, and many other factors. Consult a licensed contractor before making purchasing decisions. Energy rate projections are scenarios, not predictions.
