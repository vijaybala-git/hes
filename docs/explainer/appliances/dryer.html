Dryer: gas → heat-pump dryer

A dryer uses about the same energy per load in January as in July, so WhyWatt doesn't need weather physics here. It multiplies energy per load by loads per week. That's level 1 of 4: simple on purpose, because the simple version already gives the right answer.

Within the day

The heat-pump dryer's kWh are spread with NREL's clothes_dryer profile: mostly daytime, with some evening loads landing in the peak.

What's modeled, what's simplified

  • Your laundry habits (loads per week) drive the result.
  • Real timing of loads through the day, from NREL.
  • Panel impact of a 240 V dryer circuit is counted in the panel check.
  • Same energy every month: damp winter loads aren't modeled.
  • Energy per load is an average across load sizes and fabrics.
