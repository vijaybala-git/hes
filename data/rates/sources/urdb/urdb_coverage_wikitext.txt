The following utilities in the [[Utility Rate Database|URDB]] are updated annually. Collectively, these utilities comprise 70% of the total US electricity load. Rates for any utilities not listed below should not be assumed to reflect current tariffs. Rate update requests for utilities not listed below to support specific commercial or research projects will be considered on a case-by-case basis. The URDB could use your help! If you would like to see enhanced coverage, you can volunteer your time to update tariffs or provide feedback on the importance of the URDB to your work by emailing us.
